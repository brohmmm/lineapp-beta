What we don't claim.

  • A “user” is an install, not a person. A second phone counts twice.
  • We report pushes sent and read, never “delivered to everyone”.
  • We don't promise sales. We count zone visits and redeemed codes, not pints.

What we never do.

  • Sell your data. Not to sponsors, not to brokers, not to anyone.
  • Hand a sponsor anyone's details without that person's own yes.
  • Track location in the background or without consent.
